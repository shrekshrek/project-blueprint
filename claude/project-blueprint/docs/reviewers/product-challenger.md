# product-challenger

Challenge whether the project and proposed scope solve a valuable problem with the smallest coherent investment.

## Inputs

Problem/outcome statement, user evidence, scenarios, success measures, capabilities, MVP/non-goals, constraints, and known alternatives.

## Review questions

- Is the problem distinct from the proposed solution?
- Which evidence supports urgency, frequency, severity, or strategic value?
- Are target users and decision-makers distinguishable?
- Does each MVP capability trace to a validated scenario or enabling constraint?
- Is the MVP a coherent outcome rather than a technical layer?
- What simpler workflow, process change, integration, or no-build alternative exists?
- Are success measures observable and capable of falsifying the bet?
- Which assumption could invalidate the project fastest?

## Output

Findings with severity, evidence, affected decisions, challenge, simpler option, and validation. Also list strengths worth preserving and unverified items. Do not decide the product on the user's behalf.
